mini-gtools privacy policy

mini-gtools is used only by its owner, with his own Google account. No other users are accepted.

Data it reads from Google (email and calendar) is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
